# Frontier-7

Date: 2026-04-24

This report follows `frontier-6`.

After `frontier-6`, the remaining DER-overlap hope became more precise:

> maybe exact `11` and `12` were still too short, and the first longer surface with real pushed-token overlaps is where `FindAndDelete` finally becomes more expressive.

That is the right next question because `frontier-6` only told us that short exact DER through length `12` stayed subset-like.

It did not yet tell us whether the first overlap-bearing exact DER surface was special.

The new tool for this step is `tools/der_overlap_threshold_frontier.py`.

## Result 1: random exact-length probes stayed flat from `13` through `18`

The tool runs ordered `2`-of-`4` random family probes on exact-length DER-valid signatures for payload lengths `13` through `18`.

Results:

| Exact payload length | Trials | Unordered subset baseline | Best unique ordered outcomes | Best unordered-subset multiplicity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `13` | `5000` | `6` | `6` | `1` |
| `14` | `5000` | `6` | `6` | `1` |
| `15` | `4000` | `6` | `6` | `1` |
| `16` | `3000` | `6` | `6` | `1` |
| `17` | `2500` | `6` | `6` | `1` |
| `18` | `2000` | `6` | `6` | `1` |

So the first longer exact DER surfaces still did not produce any pairwise overlap gain in random search.

That already extends the negative frontier materially beyond `frontier-6`.

## Result 2: exact `13` also stayed flat under higher-order random search

Because exact `13` is the first truly new surface here, the tool also runs higher-order random probes on exact `13`:

- random `5`-token families with ordered `3`-of-`5` deletion
- random `6`-token families with ordered `3`-of-`6` deletion

Results:

| Surface | Trials | Unordered subset baseline | Best unique ordered outcomes | Best unordered-subset multiplicity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| exact `13`, `m=5`, `t=3` | `3000` | `10` | `10` | `1` |
| exact `13`, `m=6`, `t=3` | `2000` | `20` | `20` | `1` |

So exact `13` remained exactly subset-like even after the same higher-order lift that was used in `frontier-6`.

## Result 3: exact `13` is the first explicit overlap-bearing exact DER surface

This is the genuinely new structural point.

The tool constructs an explicit exact-`13` pool of valid DER signatures whose `s` fields embed the full pushed-token prefix fragment `0d300a0201`.

Example signatures from the pool:

- `300a02010102050d300a020103`
- `300a02010302050d300a020303`

At the pushed-token level, these produce a real proper suffix/prefix overlap:

- maximum pairwise overlap: `6`
- example overlap:
  - `overlap_a = 0d300a02010102050d300a020103`
  - `overlap_b = 0d300a02010302050d300a020303`

That matters because it breaks the older intuition that the DER path was failing only because no real overlaps existed.

At exact `13`, real overlaps do exist.

## Result 4: overlap existence still did not buy expressive power

The same explicit exact-`13` overlap pool is then searched exhaustively.

### Ordered `2`-of-`4`

- pool size: `6`
- family size: `4`
- unordered subset baseline: `6`
- best unique ordered outcomes: `6`
- best unordered-subset multiplicity: `1`

### Ordered `3`-of-`5`

- pool size: `6`
- family size: `5`
- unordered subset baseline: `10`
- best unique ordered outcomes: `10`
- best unordered-subset multiplicity: `1`

So even the first explicit overlap-bearing exact DER family remains exactly subset-like under both pairwise and higher-order deletion.

That is the key result of this frontier step:

**overlap availability is not enough.**

The first exact DER surface with real pushed-token overlaps still does not translate those overlaps into extra `FindAndDelete` states.

## Result 5: this changes what the repo should optimize

The cumulative picture is now:

- minimal `9`-byte DER is structurally dead
- exact `10`-byte DER is structurally dead
- exact `11` and `12` stayed flat under pairwise and higher-order probes
- exact `13` is the first explicit overlap-bearing exact DER surface
- but exact `13` still stays subset-like, even on an overlap-heavy constructed family
- random exact-length probes stayed flat through `18`

That is a more important shift than just extending the length bound.

It suggests that the repo should stop treating "find the first DER surface with overlaps" as the main objective.

We now have evidence that:

1. short DER can fail because no overlaps exist;
2. longer DER can still fail even when overlaps do exist.

So the likely blocker is deeper than mere cross-bifix availability.

## What is left

The next worthwhile moves are narrower:

1. materially longer or structurally different valid selectable blob families, instead of spending more cycles on DER overlap geometry;
2. a symbolic or mechanized explanation for why overlap-bearing DER families still collapse to subset behavior;
3. a new verification surface that does not rely on overlap-coded `FindAndDelete` as the main entropy lever.

That is where the repo should go next.
