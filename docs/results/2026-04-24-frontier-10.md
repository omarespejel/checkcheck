# Frontier-10

Date: 2026-04-24

This report follows `frontier-9`.

`frontier-9` moved the mechanism story forward in an important way:

- cascade is not necessary for `FindAndDelete` expressivity;
- destructive conflict looked like the broader mechanism;
- overlap-bearing exact-`13` DER families still showed no destructive conflict.

That still left a stronger open question:

> can a conflict-free family ever beat the plain subset baseline in the searched regimes, or does every expressive family need real destructive conflict?

The new tool for this step is `tools/fad_conflict_collapse_frontier.py`.

It pushes on that question in two directions:

1. widen the exhaustive toy search to mixed-length universes far beyond the earlier fixed-length probes;
2. re-check the explicit overlap-bearing exact-`13` DER pool against the plain subset baseline.

That is the cleanest current evidence for the repo’s working conjecture:

**conflict-free families collapse back to subset behavior in the searched regimes.**

## Result 3: the repo’s target changes again

At this point the progression is:

- overlap alone is not enough;
- cascade alone is not the right invariant;
- destructive conflict looks necessary in every exhaustive expressive family we have found;
- the best explicit overlap-bearing DER pool remains conflict-free and exactly subset-like.

So the next worthwhile optimization target is no longer:

> can we get more overlap?

It is:

> can we find a valid selectable blob family with explicit destructive conflict structure?

And if the answer keeps looking like no, then `FindAndDelete`-based overlap probably stops being the right frontier altogether.

## What is left

The next serious moves are:

1. search for valid blob families that are deliberately **conflict-bearing**, not just overlap-bearing;
2. push the conflict-boundary search onto longer or structurally different selectable blobs;
3. if valid conflict-bearing families still do not materialize, move the repo away from `FindAndDelete` overlap and toward a different verification surface.
